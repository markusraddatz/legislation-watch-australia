About Legislation Watch

This is an independent informational prototype. We surface official information about Australian legislation and public consultation windows, and link directly to official participation channels on government and parliamentary websites.

  • We do not collect opinions or host discussion on this site.
  • We do not advocate for or against any policy position.
  • All content is for reference only — not legal advice.

Our goal is to make open consultation windows visible early enough for independent voices to find them and participate through the proper official channels.
